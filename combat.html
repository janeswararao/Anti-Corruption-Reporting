Right to Information (RTI) is a powerful law that allows Indian citizens to seek information from government authorities. It promotes transparency by making public officials accountable for their actions. Through RTI, people can expose corruption, delays, and misuse of public funds. It empowers citizens to actively participate in democratic governance.

The Foreign Corrupt Practices Act (FCPA, the Act) is a United States law that prohibits U.S. firms and individuals from paying bribes to foreign officials to further business deals. Both the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) and the Department of Justice (DOJ) are responsible for enforcing the FCPA.
